Review comments

Heuristic scoring only — not a replacement for human judgment or your Code of Conduct process.

CoC-aligned rubric

  • Clarity: Reader can understand the concern without guessing.
  • Specificity: Points to location, behavior, or example — not vague “this is bad”.
  • Actionability: Suggests a next step or alternative the author can take.
  • Respect: Avoids personal attacks, sarcasm, and absolute dismissals (toxicity heuristic).
  • Inclusive tone: Prefer “we / this code” over “you always / you never”.
