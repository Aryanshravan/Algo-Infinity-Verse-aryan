What's Happening?

Nagle's Algorithm: If there is unacknowledged data in flight, buffer small chunks until an ACK is received (or buffer fills up).

Delayed ACK: When a packet is received, wait up to 200ms before sending an ACK, hoping to piggyback it on a response.

Deadlock Detected: Sender is waiting for an ACK to send the buffer. Receiver is waiting for more data to send the ACK. They wait until the Delayed ACK timer expires!
