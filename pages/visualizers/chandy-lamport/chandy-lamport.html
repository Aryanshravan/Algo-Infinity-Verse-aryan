Chandy-Lamport Distributed Snapshot

Capture a consistent global state in a distributed system where nodes only have local clocks. Nodes exchange tokens, and "Marker" messages are used to safely record node states and channel states.

Algorithm Rules:

  • Initiator: Records its own state, then sends a Marker out on all outgoing channels.
  • On First Marker Received: A node records its state, starts recording incoming messages on other channels, and sends Markers on its outgoing channels.
  • On Subsequent Markers: Node stops recording messages on that specific channel and saves the channel state.
  • Completion: Snapshot finishes when all nodes and all channels have recorded their state.
